Income
Money coming in this month. Switch the month at the top to log a different one.
Fixed bills
Recurring costs that stay roughly the same each month — rent, insurance, loan payments, subscriptions. Not for groceries or bulk buys — that's the Pantry tab.
Everyday spending
Smaller day-to-day spending that varies — eating out, entertainment, transport, one-off purchases.
Bulk & pantry buys
Household supplies bought in bulk from your regular suppliers — rice, cooking oil, cleaning supplies. Unlike Bills, these aren't monthly by default: each item tracks its own supplier and reorder window, and stays here across months until you restock it.
Spending history
Every month you've logged, with income, bills, pantry spend, everyday spending and what was left. Tap a month for the full breakdown.
